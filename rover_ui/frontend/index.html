Navigate to detection projects the live fused target (camera bearing + radar range) onto the map and drives to 2 m below it. Auto re-dispatches whenever a stable target appears. Follow tracks the live target continuously and re-plans the moment it moves, overriding the navigation in progress. Speed caps the drive speed (applies live, even mid-move). Confirm-N is how many consistent detections in a row are needed before a new/moved target is trusted (higher = steadier but slower to react; 1-10). Clicking the map navigates to a manual target instead.
